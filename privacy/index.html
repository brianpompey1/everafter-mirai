This notice explains what Ever After by Mirai collects through this website, why, and how long we keep it. It covers the website only — the agreements you sign when you book describe how we handle your booking.

What we collect

Information you give us

When you submit the date-inquiry form, we collect:

  • Your name or names, email address, and mobile phone number
  • Your preferred contact method
  • Your preferred and alternate dates, preferred time, and location or city
  • Your estimated guest count and the experience you selected
  • Your ceremony language and your communication language
  • Whether you are planning a legal wedding or a vow renewal
  • Any add-ons you marked as interesting, and anything you write in the free-text field
  • How you heard about us
  • Your consent choices

We ask only for what we need to check availability and recommend the right experience. Please don’t send sensitive information — immigration documents, financial account numbers, health information — through this form. We don’t need it and we don’t want to hold it.

Information collected automatically

We record which page you arrived on, the referring website, and any campaign tags in the link you clicked (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term). This tells us which ads and channels actually help couples find us.

Our analytics never receive your name, email address, phone number, or anything you typed into the free-text field. When we record a form error, we record which field had a problem — never what you entered in it.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and check availability for your date
  • To recommend an experience and, if you decide to book, send you an agreement and retainer link
  • To measure which ads, languages, and pages bring in couples we can actually serve
  • To send you optional marketing — only if you separately checked that box

Your inquiry consent and your marketing consent are separate. Declining marketing never affects your inquiry, and you can opt out of marketing at any time by replying STOP to a text or using the unsubscribe link in an email.
