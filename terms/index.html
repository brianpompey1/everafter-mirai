Marriage licenses

We provide marriage-license guidance, not legal advice. You obtain your own valid Florida marriage license from a county clerk and bring it, unexpired and effective, to your ceremony. Our officiant completes the officiant’s section and follows the required return procedure. The issuing clerk is the authority on current requirements, fees, waiting periods, and witness rules.

A vow renewal is a symbolic celebration of an existing marriage. It is not a new legal marriage and generally requires no new license.
